---
title: Proposal Rubric
scale: Excellent 100, Good 80, Needs Work 60, Below 40, No Evidence 0
---

| Criterion | Points | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Concept | 40 | Clarity and depth of the proposed idea. Is the concept specific, feasible, and ambitious for the timeline? |
| Research | 30 | Quality of the precedents and references cited, and how they inform the proposal |
| Writing & Presentation | 30 | Organization, clarity of the writing, and overall polish of the document |
