# Assignment Groups (grading scheme)

Weights are percentages of the final grade and should total 100.
"Drop Lowest" is how many lowest scores Canvas drops from that group.
Assignments pick their group by title in their `group:` front matter.

| Group | Weight | Drop Lowest |
|---|---|---|
| In-Class Exercises | 30 | 2 |
| Projects | 40 | |
| Final Project | 30 | |
