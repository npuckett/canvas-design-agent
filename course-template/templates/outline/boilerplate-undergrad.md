## Grading Breakdown

```html
<h2 style="margin-top: 24px;">Grading Breakdown</h2>
<p>Numerical grades may be translated into letter grades based upon the following scale:</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 16px;">Exceptional</h3>
<p><strong>90&ndash;100% A+<br /></strong>Demonstrated an exceptional degree of creative and/or logical thinking, a superior ability to organize, to analyze, and to integrate ideas, thorough knowledge of concepts and/or techniques, and exceptional skill in their application in satisfying the requirements of the course.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 16px;">Excellent</h3>
<p><strong>80&ndash;84% = A&minus;<br />85&ndash;89% = A<br /></strong>Demonstrated a high degree of creative and/or logical thinking, a superior ability to organize, to analyze, and to integrate ideas, thorough knowledge of concepts and/or techniques, and a very high degree of skill in their application in satisfying the requirements of a course.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 16px;">Good</h3>
<p><strong>70&ndash;73% = B&minus;<br />74&ndash;76% = B<br />77&ndash;79% = B+<br /></strong>Demonstrated a good evidence of creative and/or logical thinking, a good ability to organize, to analyze, and to integrate ideas, knowledge of concepts and/or techniques, and considerable skill in their application in satisfying the requirements of a course.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 16px;">Satisfactory</h3>
<p><strong>60&ndash;63% = C&minus;<br />64&ndash;66% = C<br />67&ndash;69% = C+<br /></strong>Demonstrated a satisfactory level of creative and/or logical thinking, a generally adequate grasp of the subject matter, knowledge of concepts and/or techniques and competence in their application in satisfying the requirements of a course.</p>

<p><strong>50&ndash;53% = D&minus;<br />54&ndash;56% = D<br />57&ndash;59% = D+<br /></strong>Demonstrated barely adequate knowledge and ability in creative and/or logical thinking and the application of concepts and/or techniques in satisfying the requirements of a course.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 16px;">Unsatisfactory</h3>
<p><strong>0&ndash;49% = F<br /></strong>Failed to meet minimum acceptable command of knowledge and ability in creative and/or logical thinking and the application of concepts and/or techniques in satisfying the requirements of a course; or failure to complete a course; or by sanction as permitted by the university&rsquo;s academic misconduct policy.</p>
```

## Academic Integrity

```html
<h2 style="margin-top: 24px;">Academic Integrity</h2>
<p>Academic integrity is a shared responsibility held by all members of the OCAD University community. We uphold the integrity of knowledge-making and creativity: this means obtaining and sharing information responsibly, being respectful and transparent in the way that we use and respond to the work and contributions of others and explicitly acknowledging how such work and contributions inform our own. The University is committed to enforcing the policy and all allegations of academic misconduct will therefore be reported and investigated. The most common type of academic misconduct is plagiarism, that is, deliberately misrepresenting someone else&rsquo;s words, ideas or original visual concepts as your own in written or visual work. Other examples of academic misconduct include misuse of written or visual sources (&ldquo;unintentional plagiarism&rdquo;), cheating during an examination or test, collaborating on assignments without permission, and submitting any work for evaluation that has previously been submitted in another course without prior approval. Read the <a href="https://www.ocadu.ca/about-ocad-u/our-story/policies?combine=1.1" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Academic Misconduct policy</a> to ensure you are well informed.</p>
<h3 style="margin-top: 16px;">Academic Integrity and Generative Artificial Intelligence (GAI)</h3>
<p>The Academic Misconduct policy includes specific guidance (rules/recommendations) around Generative AI (GAI) applications (e.g. Sora, Midjourney, ChatGPT, Replit). GAI can be a powerful tool for research and creative practice, but may not always be relevant or appropriate for use in a course. Use of a GAI application can be a form of academic misconduct. As a student, you have a responsibility to follow the parameters (rules) set by your instructor for the use of GAI in your course. Parameters for acceptable GAI use vary from course to course and even assignment to assignment. Consult your course outline and assignment instructions for guidance on how these applications may be used and how they should be attributed (referenced) when you do use them. When unclear or in doubt, reach out to your course instructor for clarification.</p>
```

## Commitment to Sustainability

```html
<h2 style="margin-top: 24px;">Commitment to Sustainability</h2>
<p>OCAD University is committed to developing decolonial approaches to sustainability that foster environmental justice through interdisciplinary solutions. To address the climate crisis, you are encouraged to approach your art, design, creative and critical practices with an understanding of the interconnections among environmental, social, economic and cultural activities. You may also consult the OCAD U <a href="https://www.ocadu.ca/about-ocad-u/our-story/policies?combine=13.1" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Sustainability Policy</a> which builds on the <a href="https://www.un.org/sustainabledevelopment/sustainable-development-goals/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (UNSDGs)</a> in support of ecologically-informed and holistic thinking that cultivates relationships between people, place, and land. Together we can advance global sustainability goals which include, among others: reduced inequalities; good health and well-being; affordable and clean energy; clean water and sanitation; climate action; and responsible consumption and production.</p>
```
