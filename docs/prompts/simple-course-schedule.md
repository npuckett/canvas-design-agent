## Course Description

This course introduces core design principles through short readings, studio exercises, critique, and project-based work. Students will practice composition, visual hierarchy, typography, color, and iterative making while building a small portfolio of design studies.

No prior design experience is required. Students should bring curiosity, a sketchbook, and a willingness to revise their work.

## Learning Goals

- Identify and apply basic principles of visual design
- Use critique language to describe design decisions clearly
- Develop projects through sketches, drafts, feedback, and revision
- Present finished work with concise written reflection
